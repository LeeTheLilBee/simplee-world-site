The company behind the work

We don't see software as a collection of disconnected apps. We design systems that can share useful context while retaining their own responsibilities, permissions, and boundaries.

Simplee World brings together engineering, operational research, and a long-term vision for technology-enabled economic and community infrastructure. The present focus is building and validating the software foundation, not presenting unfinished products as commercially available.

01 / The ecosystem

Different systems.

These are specific workstreams under development. Descriptions explain their intended roles; they do not represent publicly launched products or imply third-party certification.

01 / IntelligenceIn development

The Observatory

A market-intelligence and research environment for collecting provider-specific financial data, normalizing it for analysis, evaluating indicators and conditions, and supporting informed review of trading ideas.

Engineering focusIndependent provider integrations · Quote integrity · Signal evaluation · Paper-account simulation · Review workflows

Research and evaluation tooling; not a claim of live automated trading, returns, or financial advice.

02 / Applied intelligenceIn development

Soulaana

An intelligence component being developed to make complicated technical and operational findings understandable—helping explain evidence, decisions, uncertainty, and outcomes across the ecosystem.

Engineering focusHuman-readable explanations · Evidence-based reasoning · Decision feedback · Privacy-conscious learning boundaries
03 / Access & oversightIn development

The Tower

The intended front door for identity, roles, access requests, and owner oversight. Its purpose is to make clear who can see or do what across different services without weakening their boundaries.

Engineering focusIdentity · Permissions · Approval workflows · Operational visibility · Separation of duties
04 / Data resilienceIn development

The Vault

A secure storage and recovery layer focused on protecting important files, evidence, and operational records with permission-aware access and dependable recovery practices.

Engineering focusEncryption · Controlled access · Backup verification · Restorability · Recovery drills
05 / OperationsPlanned & in development

The Teller

Operational finance and administrative workflows for tracking obligations, coordinating approvals, organizing records, and making day-to-day decisions easier to understand.

Engineering focusFinancial workflows · Controls · Administrative records · Review queues · Reporting

No claim that the company currently provides regulated financial or payroll services.

06 / DiscoveryIn development

BuyBox

A research and discovery workstream for surfacing potential property opportunities from public evidence, tracking changes in status, and organizing information for later human evaluation.

Engineering focusPublic-record discovery · Source verification · Status tracking · Document processing

Research workflow only; this is not a claim of active acquisitions or brokerage services.

32 GB+ developer workstation

A Linux-compatible laptop, mini PC, or desktop with 32 GB or more RAM, capable multicore CPU, and ideally 1 TB SSD to run local services, databases, and repeatable integration tests.

02 / RECOVERY

Two independent backup SSDs

Two reliable 1–2 TB external SSDs for separate encrypted backup copies, restore testing, and project archives. Two copies protect us from relying on one device.

03 / SUSTAINABILITY

Developer cloud capacity

Compute and database credits for integration testing, staging, and constrained hosting, with transparent costs and migration requirements after any credit expires.

We can discuss an honest, clearly disclosed technical evaluation of gifted hardware using genuine development workflows. A permanent gift is preferred over a temporary review loan. No endorsement, product coverage, or partnership is implied before agreement.
