The company behind the vision

Simplee World is a founder-led company creating businesses designed to work for people and communities over time. We want to pair practical services with responsible technology and long-term investment.

Our development work starts with strong foundations: thoughtful business design, dependable operations, and software that helps keep important work organized. We are sharing our direction—not making claims that planned ventures are already open to the public.

THE INFRASTRUCTURE THAT MOVES THE WORK FORWARDINDEPENDENT DEVELOPER / 2026
01 / COMPUTE

32 GB+ developer workstation

A Linux-compatible laptop, mini PC, or desktop with 32 GB or more RAM, capable multicore CPU, and ideally 1 TB SSD to run local services, databases, and repeatable integration tests.

02 / RECOVERY

Two independent backup SSDs

Two reliable 1–2 TB external SSDs for separate encrypted backup copies, restore testing, and project archives. Two copies protect us from relying on one device.

03 / SUSTAINABILITY

Developer cloud capacity

Compute and database credits for integration testing, staging, and constrained hosting, with transparent costs and migration requirements after any credit expires.

We can discuss an honest, clearly disclosed technical evaluation of gifted hardware using genuine development workflows. A permanent gift is preferred over a temporary review loan. No endorsement, product coverage, or partnership is implied before agreement.
